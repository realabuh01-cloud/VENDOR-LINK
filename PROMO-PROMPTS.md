## ⚙️ How to use these prompts (important)

1. **Generate scene-by-scene.** Most tools make 5–10s clips per prompt. Each
   video below is split into 4–5 short scenes — generate each separately,
   then stitch them in **CapCut (free)**.
2. **AI video tools are bad at text.** Keep on-screen words minimal. Add the
   logo, tagline and captions afterwards in CapCut (it takes 2 minutes and
   looks 10x better).
3. **Aspect ratios:** use **9:16 (vertical)** for TikTok/Reels/WhatsApp
   status (your audience!), **1:1** for Instagram feed, **16:9** for
   YouTube/landing-page hero.
4. **Same seed style:** start every prompt with the same style line —
   *"cinematic, natural warm African sunlight, emerald green and white color
   palette, shallow depth of field, realistic, no text"* — so the clips cut
   together seamlessly.

## 🎬 VIDEO A — "Her Shop, Her Phone, Her Empire" (hero teaser, 30s total)

**Hook:** one woman's market stall becomes a 24/7 online store.

### Scene A1 (0–6s) — The stall
> Cinematic vertical 9:16 shot, natural warm African sunlight. A young Nigerian
> woman in a colorful Lagos market stall arranges stacks of vibrant ankara
> fabric, wooden stall beams above her, morning light streaming in. She glances
> down at her smartphone, curious. Realistic documentary style, shallow depth
> of field, emerald green fabric accents in the frame, no text.

### Scene A2 (6–12s) — The tap
> Close-up macro shot of a thumb tapping a glowing emerald green button on a
> clean white mobile app screen that reads "Create your free store". The phone
> is held by a dark-skinned Nigerian woman, colorful market blurred in the
> background. Soft screen glow on her face, bokeh background, cinematic,
> realistic, vertical 9:16, no other text.

### Scene A3 (12–18s) — The storefront appears
> Over-the-shoulder shot: the phone screen smoothly transforms into a polished
> online storefront — a grid of product photos, Naira prices, a green "Order
> via WhatsApp" button and a cyan "Pay now" button sliding in. The woman's eyes
> widen with pride. Emerald and white UI colors glow softly in a dimmed market
> stall, cinematic lighting, vertical 9:16.

### Scene A4 (18–24s) — Orders buzz in
> The woman laughs joyfully as her phone lights up with incoming order
> notifications, small green bubbles appearing over the screen, while a
> customer hands her cash and receives a fabric bag in return. Busy but warm
> market atmosphere, motion energy, candid documentary style, vertical 9:16.

### Scene A5 (24–30s) — The closer
> Final shot: the woman holds her phone toward the camera, screen glowing
> emerald with a simple white storefront icon, she smiles proudly, market
> bokeh behind her. The scene brightens to a clean emerald gradient. Cinematic,
> hopeful, vertical 9:16, no text — leave space at the bottom for the
> "VendorLink" logo and tagline added in editing.

## 🪪 LOGO PROMPTS (copy into Midjourney, Ideogram, DALL·E, Leonardo)

### Main app icon (use everywhere)
> Minimalist modern app icon logo for "VendorLink", an African e-commerce
> platform where vendors create instant online storefronts. A rounded-square
> emerald green icon containing a simple white storefront awning mark, the
> awning's second stripe subtly continuing into a small chain-link shape to
> suggest "link". Flat vector style, clean geometric shapes, generous
> whitespace, no gradients, no 3D, no bevel, no photorealism, no text, solid
> white background, centered, scalable, readable at 16px.

### Wordmark version (for website header)
> Flat vector logo design on white background: a bold lowercase wordmark
> "vendorlink" in dark slate, with the letters "link" in emerald green
> (#059669), and a small white storefront-awning glyph sitting inside an
> emerald rounded square to the left of the wordmark. Modern sans-serif,
> clean spacing, minimal, no other text, no gradients, no 3D.

### Monochrome version (for print/receipts/whatsapp)
> The same minimal storefront-awning logo mark in solid black on a white
> background, flat vector, no color, no text, high contrast, crisp edges.

### Favicon / tiny mark
> A tiny 64x64 app favicon: a single white storefront awning shape centered
> on an emerald green rounded square, flat design, no text, no detail,
> legible at very small size.

### Negative prompts (add to any image tool)
> no 3D, no bevel, no glossy shine, no photorealism, no gradients, no letters,
> no watermark, no extra shapes, no busy background

## ✂️ Post-production checklist (CapCut, free)

1. Stitch scenes in order with 0.3s crossfades
2. Add text: opener "Your online store, live in minutes" → closer
   "VendorLink" + "Free forever · No fees"
3. Music: upbeat Afrobeats instrumental, duck audio at the closer
4. End card: emerald background + logo + tagline + "vendorlink.ng"
5. Export: 1080x1920 (vertical) or 1080x1080 / 1920x1080 per platform
