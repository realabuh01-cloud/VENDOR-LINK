# VendorLink — COMBINED one-shot video prompts 🎬

Each prompt below is a single continuous 30-second (or shorter) film.
Copy-paste the whole block into a long-form video tool (Sora, Veo 3,
Kling 1.6+). If your tool caps at 5–10s per generation, use the same
text as the master style + first scene, then continue scene-by-scene —
the style lock keeps the cuts seamless.

**Style lock (already baked into every prompt):**
cinematic, natural warm African sunlight, emerald green (#059669) and
white color palette, realistic documentary look, shallow depth of field,
no on-screen text (add logo/tagline in editing).

---

## 🎬 VIDEO A — "Her Shop, Her Phone, Her Empire" (30s, vertical 9:16)

> A continuous 30-second cinematic vertical 9:16 film, realistic documentary
> style, natural warm African sunlight, emerald green and white color
> palette, shallow depth of field, no text.
>
> Open in a bustling Lagos market at morning: a young Nigerian woman in a
> colorful headwrap arranges stacks of vibrant ankara fabric in her wooden
> market stall, warm sunlight streaming between the beams. She notices her
> smartphone on the counter and picks it up. Cut to a close-up of her thumb
> tapping a glowing emerald green button on a clean white app that reads
> "Create your free store", her face softly lit by the screen, market bokeh
> behind her. The screen transforms into a polished online storefront — a
> grid of product photos with Naira prices, a green "Order via WhatsApp"
> button and a cyan "Pay now" button sliding in — and her eyes widen with
> pride. The scene bursts into life: customers approach, she hands over
> fabric bags, and her phone lights up with incoming green order bubbles.
> Final shot: golden-hour light, she closes her stall and walks toward
> camera holding her phone out, screen glowing emerald with a simple white
> storefront icon, smiling proudly, the market fading into a clean emerald
> gradient behind her. Hopeful, energetic, celebratory Afrobeats energy,
> seamless single narrative, vertical 9:16.

## ⚙️ If your tool caps at ~10 seconds per clip

1. Use the video's **style-lock line** as the opening of every scene prompt
2. Regenerate scene-by-scene (the scene prompts live in `PROMO-PROMPTS.md`)
3. Keep the **same seed/style reference image** if the tool supports it
4. Stitch in CapCut with 0.3s crossfades — the locked style makes it look
   like one take
